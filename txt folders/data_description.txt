2. engine_fault_detection_dataset.csv

Dataset Purpose
- Designed to detect and classify engine faults using signal processing and machine learning.
- Contains sensor data under normal, minor fault, and critical fault conditions.
Features
- 11 independent variables (sensor readings)

1 dependent variable: Engine_Condition (0: Normal, 1: Minor Fault, 2: Critical Fault)
| **Feature**             | **Description**                                    | **Unit**        | **Range**  |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ---------- |
| `Vibration_Amplitude`   | Peak engine vibration amplitude                    | mm/s²           | 0.1 – 10.0 |
| `RMS_Vibration`         | Root Mean Square of vibration                      | mm/s²           | 0.05 – 5.0 |
| `Vibration_Frequency`   | Frequency of engine vibration                      | Hz              | 20 – 2000  |
| `Surface_Temperature`   | Engine surface temperature                         | °C              | 30 – 150   |
| `Exhaust_Temperature`   | Exhaust gas temperature                            | °C              | 200 – 600  |
| `Acoustic_dB`           | Acoustic noise level                               | dB              | 60 – 120   |
| `Acoustic_Frequency`    | Frequency of engine noise                          | Hz              | 100 – 5000 |
| `Intake_Pressure`       | Intake manifold pressure                           | kPa             | 90 – 120   |
| `Exhaust_Pressure`      | Exhaust gas pressure                               | kPa             | 80 – 110   |
| `Frequency_Band_Energy` | Energy in a specific frequency band (STFT-derived) | Arbitrary Units | 0.1 – 1.0  |
| `Amplitude_Mean`        | Mean signal amplitude over time                    | Arbitrary Units | 0.01 – 0.5 |


3. Multiple_Classification_EV_Battery.csv & Simple_Classification_EV_Battery_Faults_Dataset.csv
Two datasets have been developed based on battery modeling in MATLAB. Both datasets share the same input features:
o State of Charge (SOC)
o Temperature
o Voltage

Each dataset differs in its output label interpretation:

--> Dataset 1: Binary Battery Health Classification
Purpose: Classify whether the battery is healthy or unhealthy.

Output Label:

0 → Healthy Battery
1 → Unhealthy Battery

--> Dataset 2: Multi-Class Battery Fault Classification
Purpose: Identify specific types of battery faults.

Output Label:

0 → Healthy Battery
1 → Over-Discharge Fault
2 → Short-Circuit Fault
